# Safe-mirror methodology

The Corporate Wallet Digital Twin models the identity `A = qT`, where the safe fixture observes anonymized aggregate activity `A` and treats total wallet `T` and share `q` as latent. It reports identification bounds, model-based posterior intervals and explicitly labelled commercial scenarios. The Decision Twin maps an interval to a role, problem, solution, timing and discovery question. The Promotion Twin evaluates REAL and REHEARSAL evidence separately.

This mirror contains independently generated `D01`–`D20` aggregates and public-source registries only. It establishes software mechanics, not the confidential hackathon result, measured competitor share, bank economics, RM adoption, causal uplift or bank authorization.
